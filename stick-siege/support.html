Troubleshooting

The "watch an ad to double" button says the ad is unavailable
Rewarded ads are optional and come from Google AdMob. They can be unavailable when there is no connection, when no ad is available in your region at that moment, or right after a fresh install. Your battle reward is already yours; only the bonus needs the ad. Tap the button again later, or carry on.
I watched the ad but did not get the bonus
The bonus is granted only when the ad reports that it was watched to the end. If you closed it early, nothing is deducted and you can try again. If it played to the end and the bonus did not appear, email us with the time it happened.
My progress is gone
Stick Siege has no accounts. Your levels, upgrades, gold bars, looks and Last Stand army are saved only on your device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to another device. We cannot restore them for you. A device backup (iCloud or a computer backup) that includes app data is the only way to carry a save over.
Do I need to "restore purchases"?
No. This release of Stick Siege is free and has nothing to buy, so there is nothing to restore. If a paid full-game unlock is added in a future version, it will be handled through Apple and restorable in the usual way.
The game is stuck, slow or will not open
Close it fully and reopen it. If it still misbehaves, restart the device. If that fails, email us the device model, iOS version and what you were doing. Please do not delete the app first if you want to keep your progress.
Ad privacy choices
Inside the game, Settings has an "Ad privacy choices" entry where required by your region. Tracking permission is controlled in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Saying no never costs you a reward.
