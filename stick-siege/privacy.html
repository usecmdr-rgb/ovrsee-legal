Stick Siege ✦ Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Stick Siege is made by OVRSEE LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Stick Siege mobile app.

The short version. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or address. Your progress is saved on your own device. What leaves the device is limited to what the optional ads need to work and, if we have turned it on, anonymous gameplay statistics.

What stays on your device

Your saved game — levels cleared, upgrades, gold bars, looks, your Last Stand army and your sound settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. We cannot read it, it is never uploaded, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Stick Siege shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You are never made to watch one: an ad appears only when you choose to tap a button to double a battle reward, and declining costs you nothing but the bonus.

Before the first ad, iOS may ask whether you allow tracking. Whatever you answer, you still get the reward and the game still works. Saying no means ads are chosen without an advertising identifier — the same ads, just less relevant ones.

Google may use a device identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address and data about the ads you see to select and measure advertising. How Google uses this is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in Europe or the UK

Where required, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before any ad is shown, and records your choice. You can change it at any time in the game's Settings under "Ad privacy choices".

Our legal basis is your consent for advertising, and our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played for any anonymous gameplay statistics. You have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising may count as a "sale" or "share" under some state laws. You can opt out by declining the tracking request iOS shows you, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You have the right to know what has been collected and to ask us to delete it.

Children

Stick Siege is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Anonymous gameplay statistics, where collected, are kept for up to 24 months. If purchases are ever offered, their records would be kept as long as needed to honour a restore and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

Where your data goes

Our service providers are based in the United States and the European Union, so data may be transferred outside your country. Transfers out of the EEA and UK rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your choices, in one place

  • Stop ad tracking — decline the iOS tracking request, or turn it off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change your ad consent (Europe/UK) — in-game Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Delete everything on your device — delete the app.
  • Ask us to delete what we hold — email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
