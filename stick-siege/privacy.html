Stick Siege — Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Stick Siege is made by OVRSEE LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Stick Siege mobile app.

The short version: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, or address. Your progress is stored on your own device. The only data that leaves the device is anonymous gameplay statistics and whatever the advertising and payment services need to work.

What stays on your device

Your saved game — levels cleared, upgrades, gold bars, cosmetics, campaign progress, and your sound settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. We cannot read it, it is never uploaded, and deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

WhatWhyWho handles it
A random ID created when you first open the game To count players and sessions without identifying anyone. It is not linked to you, your device, or any account, and a fresh install creates a new one. PostHog
Gameplay events — a level started or finished, a purchase in the in-game shop, an ad watched, the tutorial completed To see which levels are too hard, what players actually use, and where they stop playing. PostHog

Advertising

Stick Siege shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. You are never made to watch one: they appear only when you choose to tap a button to double a reward, and declining costs you nothing but the bonus.

On first showing an ad, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. Whatever you answer, you still get the reward and the game still works. Saying no means the ads you see are chosen without an advertising identifier — you get the same number of ads, just less relevant ones.

Google may use a device identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and data about the ads you see to select and measure advertising. How Google uses this is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in Europe or the UK

Before any ad is shown, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form, and records your choice. You can change it at any time: open Settings inside the game and tap Manage on "Ad privacy choices".

Our legal basis is your consent for advertising, and our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played for the anonymous gameplay statistics. You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your national data protection authority.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising may count as a "sale" or "share" under some state laws. You can opt out by declining the tracking request iOS shows you, or by turning on "Limit Ad Tracking" in your device settings. You have the right to know what we have collected, and to ask us to delete it.

Children

Stick Siege is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Anonymous gameplay statistics are kept for up to 24 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honour a restore and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

Where your data goes

Our service providers are based in the United States and the European Union, so data may be transferred outside your country. Transfers out of the EEA and UK rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your choices, in one place

  • Stop ad tracking — decline the iOS tracking request, or turn it off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change your ad consent (Europe/UK) — in-game Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Delete everything on your device — delete the app.
  • Ask us to delete what we hold — email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
